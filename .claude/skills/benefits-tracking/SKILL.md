# benefits-tracking

## Purpose
Keeps benefits measurable and owned.

## When to use
- Benefits review or milestone reached

## Inputs
- Benefits register and actuals

## Process
1. Verify measure, baseline, target, owner.
2. Update forecast and actuals.
3. Flag shortfalls and dependencies.

## Output
Updated benefits register and summary.
